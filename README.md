📊 Car Sales Dashboard – Overview & Details

This project presents an interactive Car Sales Dashboard built to analyze and visualize key performance metrics across the automotive sales domain. The dashboard is structured into two main views — Overview and Details — each providing insights through dynamic charts, tables, and filters.

🚗 Dashboard – Overview Page

The Overview page delivers a high-level summary of sales performance with visually rich analytics:

🔢 YTD Total Sales, Avg Price & Cars Sold
Displays year-to-date totals, month-to-date values, and growth percentages through highlighted KPI cards.

📈 Weekly Sales Trend
A line graph showcasing week-by-week sales performance helping identify seasonal or weekly fluctuations.

🟣 Sales Distribution by Body Style & Color
Pie and donut charts represent how different car body styles and colors contribute to total YTD sales.

🗺 YOY Sales by Dealer Region
An interactive map showing dealer performance across various U.S. regions.

🏢 Company-wise Sales Trend
A tabular bar-chart hybrid displaying average price, YTD cars sold, total sales, and YOY growth for major car manufacturers.
